# 📊 Data Pulse — Sales Dashboard

An interactive sales analytics dashboard built using Power BI to analyze revenue, orders, units sold, product categories, regional performance, and customer ratings.

## 📌 Project Overview

Data Pulse is an interactive Power BI sales analytics project designed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides an executive overview, detailed sales analysis, and category-level drill-through analysis to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Total revenue reached approximately **$5.55M**.
- **Electronics** generated the highest category revenue at approximately **$2.2M**.
- The **East** region recorded the highest revenue at approximately **$1.53M**.
- Revenue peaked around **July**, followed by a significant decline around September.
- The overall average customer rating was approximately **4.0/5**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing Electronics products through inventory planning and targeted promotions.
- Investigate the lower revenue performance of the West region.
- Analyze the factors behind the July revenue peak and replicate successful strategies.
- Investigate the sharp September revenue decline.
- Maintain product and service quality to sustain the strong average customer rating.
