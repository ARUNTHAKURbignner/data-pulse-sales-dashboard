# 📊 DataPulse Sales Dashboard

An interactive sales analytics dashboard built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze revenue, orders, units sold, product categories, regional performance, and customer ratings.

## 📌 Project Overview

DataPulse is a Power BI sales analytics project designed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides an executive overview, detailed sales analysis, and category-level drill-through analysis to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **Electronics** is the highest-revenue category.
- The **East** region generated the highest regional revenue.
- Monthly revenue shows noticeable variation throughout the year.
- Revenue reaches a strong peak around **July**.
- The overall average customer rating is approximately **4.0/5**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing Electronics products through inventory planning and targeted promotions.
- Investigate the lower revenue performance of the West region.
- Analyze the factors behind the July revenue peak and replicate successful strategies.
- Investigate the September revenue decline.
- Maintain product and service quality to sustain customer satisfaction.
