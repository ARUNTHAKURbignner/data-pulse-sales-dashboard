# DataPulse Sales Dashboard — Project Notes

## 1. Project Overview

DataPulse is an interactive sales analytics dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The purpose of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive business intelligence solution that helps users understand sales performance across different categories, regions, time periods, and customer ratings.

The dashboard is designed to provide both high-level business performance and detailed analytical views.

---

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Monitor total revenue and units sold.
- Track total orders.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Monitor customer ratings.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing categories.
- Identify regional sales opportunities.
- Provide interactive filtering for business users.
- Enable category-level drill-through analysis.
- Present insights in a clear and professional dashboard.

## 10. Key Business Insights

### Insight 1 — Overall Revenue

The business generated approximately $5.55M in total revenue across 600 orders.

This indicates a strong overall sales performance.

### Insight 2 — Category Performance

Electronics is the highest-revenue category, contributing approximately $2.2M.

This makes Electronics an important category for revenue generation.

### Insight 3 — Regional Performance

The East region generated the highest regional revenue at approximately $1.53M.

The West region generated comparatively lower revenue and may represent an opportunity for improvement.

### Insight 4 — Monthly Revenue

Revenue varies significantly across months, with a strong peak around July followed by a noticeable decline around September.

This indicates that seasonal or campaign-related factors may influence sales performance.

### Insight 5 — Customer Ratings

The overall average customer rating is approximately 4.0 out of 5.

This indicates generally positive customer satisfaction.

---

## 11. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

### Recommendation 1

Prioritize high-performing Electronics products through targeted promotions and inventory planning.

### Recommendation 2

Investigate the lower revenue performance of the West region and identify factors such as product demand, pricing, marketing, or distribution.

### Recommendation 3

Analyze the factors responsible for the July revenue peak and consider replicating successful campaigns during similar periods.

### Recommendation 4

Investigate the significant revenue decline around September to identify potential operational or seasonal causes.

### Recommendation 5

Continue monitoring customer ratings and maintain product and service quality to sustain customer satisfaction.
